Building the tools to program living systems.

Every living cell already carries the instructions to build a heart, close a wound, and grow a body from a single egg. Life is the most sophisticated technology that has ever existed. We have simply never had the tools to read it, write to it, and direct it on purpose.

The right tools will turn biology from something we study into something we engineer. The same instructions that let an embryo build a body can let a body rebuild itself: regenerating organs instead of waiting on donors, reversing disease instead of slowing it, and treating aging not as the fixed price of being alive, but as a biological process that can be understood and ultimately rewritten.

This is the work of orchestrating intelligent life, and we are doing it with the best people in the world: the scientists, engineers, and builders who are the best at what they do. Together we are laying the foundation for a century in which health is something we sustain rather than slowly lose.
